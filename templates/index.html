About The Project

Crop yield estimation has historically been vulnerable to volatile weather patterns and unmonitored soil variations. Agri Yield Predictor models the non-linear biological response curves of 10 staple and commercial crops—including Rice, Wheat, Maize, Cotton, Sugarcane, Soybean, Potato, Groundnut, Tomato, and Barley.

Disclaimer: This prediction is an estimate based on available agricultural data and should be used as a decision-support tool, not as a guarantee of actual crop yield.
